# VoteWise

## What This Is

VoteWise is a minimalist voter alignment tool that helps users determine their alignment with political candidates through focused surveys and objective data. It prioritizes a calm, distraction-free experience to facilitate civic decision-making.

## Core Value

Objective, distraction-free determination of voter alignment with candidates.
